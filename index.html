Built for the way teams work.

I'm a security practitioner focused on cloud, product, and detection engineering. My work starts by listening: to the people building the system, the people using it, and the evidence it leaves behind.

I turn that context into practical improvements: a clearer threat model, a smaller blast radius, a useful alert, or an incident plan that works when the pressure is real.
